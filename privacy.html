Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy describes the Studyfoldr iPhone app and this website (studyfoldr.com). Studyfoldr is made by Edward Dunn.

No account

Studyfoldr does not require an account. There is no cloud account to create, and you do not sign in to use the app.

What stays on your device

Decks, cards, notes, and scores are stored on your device. Studyfoldr does not collect personal data from the app for our own records, and those study materials are not uploaded to a Studyfoldr server.

No tracking, analytics, or ads

The app does not include trackers, third-party analytics, or advertising. We do not sell your data.

Offline by design

The app’s features do not require a network connection. You can study without being online.

This website

studyfoldr.com is a static marketing site. It does not collect form data. There is no contact form that posts information to a server. If we later add a form, this policy will be updated to describe what is collected and why.

Like most websites, your browser may send standard technical requests (such as your IP address) to the host that serves these pages. That is ordinary web hosting behavior; this site itself does not add tracking pixels or analytics scripts.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@studyfoldr.com.

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted on this page with a new last-updated date.
