How the app works

  • Library — Decks, cards, and notes are stored on your device so you can browse and study without signing in.
  • Study Split — Notes and flashcards sit side by side so you can read and review in one view.
  • Face-to-Face Quiz — Two people quiz each other across two panes, with an optional flip/rotation for opposing seats and a simple score counter.

Studyfoldr works on iPhone Duo and regular iPhone. The app’s features do not require a network connection.

Where your data lives

Decks, notes, and scores live on the device. There is no Studyfoldr cloud account. If you delete the app, local decks and notes are removed with it unless you have made your own backup outside the app.
